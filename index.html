The Rebirth of Hunger Games

Rebirth Games is a Hunger Games-inspired simulation where 24 tributes from 12 districts compete to become the last survivor. Players can create their own characters and customize their stats, including IQ, speed, strength, durability, awareness, confidence, and aggressiveness, to influence the outcome. Each tribute's stats affect their decisions, combat abilities, dodging, hunting, and alliances. Different motivations, strategic choices, and random events make every simulation unique and unpredictable. By adjusting character stats, players can explore different scenarios and discover how intelligence, strength, speed, and decision-making influence each tribute's chances of survival.
